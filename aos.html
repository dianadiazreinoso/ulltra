Entity Resolution

A of your operations.

Every institution runs on entities — orders, batches, suppliers, decisions — scattered across systems that do not agree on what they are. AOS resolves them into one ontology: the shared semantic layer where data, logic, and action meet. Agents that cannot agree on identity cannot be trusted with operations. AOS solves identity first.

From data to
decision to action.

Dashboards describe the world. AOS operates in it. Every AOS application runs the same loop — and every pass through it makes the ontology, and the decisions built on it, better.

  • Ingest

    Collector agents

    Pull from APIs, files, DB replicas and phone-call transcription. Schema drift detected, not assumed away.

  • Resolve

    Entity resolution

    Deterministic + probabilistic matching reconciles identifiers across systems into canonical entities.

  • Model

    Knowledge graph

    Facts land in a bitemporal, ontology-typed graph. Queries answer "what did we know, and when."

  • Reason

    Agent teams

    Agents traverse the graph, retrieve precedent, and propose decisions — with cited evidence, never on vibes.

  • Act

    Governed action

    Validated decisions are written back to your operational systems. Every action carries its evidence, its authors — human and agent — and its full trace.
