Lista Robinson is Adigital's advertising opt-out service: consumers register once to stop receiving commercial calls and emails, and every direct-marketing company operating in Spain is legally required to check it before each campaign. With six million people registered — twelve percent of the country — it is one of the most successful opt-out lists in the world.

01 — The challenge

The problem with the old model

A national opt-out list is a paradox: to protect people's data, you build the most sensitive marketing database in the country — and then let thousands of companies query it. Every query was a potential exposure; trust rested on policy, not on mathematics.

02 — The solution

The rebuild

We redesigned the service as a set of APIs where exposure is impossible by construction. Companies check their campaign lists against cryptographic identifiers and receive a yes or a no — nothing more. Security and reliability improved for everyone: consumers, companies and the regulator.

The list answers questions. It never reveals people.

Lista Robinson is one of the most significant applied-cryptography projects built in Spain. A zero-knowledge model lets companies verify list membership without ever accessing the personal data behind it.
