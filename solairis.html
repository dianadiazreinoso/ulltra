A rooftop is more than an image. It is the of constraints.

Geometry determines what fits. Consumption determines what pays. Regulation determines what gets funded. A project exists only where those constraints intersect. Each one lives in a different dataset, under a different identifier, maintained by a different institution. The hard problem isn't detection—it's entity resolution: binding every constraint to the same physical property.

Geometry

What the roof can hold

LIDAR point clouds yield the true three-dimensional geometry of every roof plane; cadastral spatial geometries anchor it to the parcel; solar geometry over both yields the shading function of every plane, every hour of the year.

  • LIDAR-derived roof geometries
  • cadastral spatial geometries & footprints
  • rooftop segmentation (CV over orthophotos)
  • orientation, tilt & obstacle extraction
  • hourly irradiance & shading model
Load

What the building will pay for

A production curve without a consumption curve is astronomy, not economics. Real demand data closes the model: self-consumption ratio, sizing, and the counterfactual bill.

  • smart meter curves (Datadis)
  • supply-point registry (SIPS)
  • energy certificate databases
  • tariff structures & market prices
Incentives

What the state will co-fund

Subsidy eligibility is constraint satisfaction over a corpus that changes weekly — programmes open, budgets exhaust, deadlines pass. Solairis evaluates it continuously, per site.

  • national subsidy registry (BDNS)
  • regional & municipal programmes
  • eligibility as constraint matching
  • budget-exhaustion & deadline tracking

Enumerate. Resolve. Infer. Synthesize. Dispatch.

Prospecting samples a territory at random. Solairis computes over all of it — then sends people only where the posterior justifies the trip.

Enumerate

The full search space

Every rooftop in the territory, extracted and parameterized: surface, planes, orientation, obstacles. Nothing sampled — everything counted.

Resolve

Identity across silos

Entity resolution binds cadastre, supply point, consumption curve and eligible programmes to one physical roof. Portfolio intake (Excel/CSV) joins the same graph.

Infer

Expected value, per site

Technical fit, economic return and closing probability compose into a single ranking function. The ordering is calibrated, not curated.

Synthesize

The agentic engineer

An engineering agent derives the complete project per ranked site — layout, production, savings, subsidies — from the resolved data. Minutes, not weeks.

Dispatch

Routes as optimization

The ranked pipeline compiles into daily field routes — a vehicle-routing problem solved against expected value per door, not distance alone.

The study is not a document. It is a derivation.

An agentic engineer performs the project automatically: it reads the resolved data of each site and derives the complete engineering study from it — no templates, no typical values. In our first pilots this synthesis already exceeds 58% measured accuracy against ground truth, and the agents that produce it retrain their own outputs, so the figure improves day by day.
